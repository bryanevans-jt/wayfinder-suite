Wayfinder Pro — Employment Specialist Dashboard

Companion reference for ES training videos and hands-on onboarding. Use the ES training demo (Portal → Settings → Demos → Launch ES training demo) for screenshots and practice.

1. Getting started

Employment Specialists work primarily in Clients, Messages, and Billable Hours. Navigation matches production for your organization’s enabled features (community partners, analytics, exports, and so on).

Training demo clients are anonymized, flagged is_demo, and excluded from counselor dashboards, analytics, and compliance metrics.
Screenshot: ES training demo — Clients home with Today strip and application pipeline

2. Clients caseload

Today strip & pipeline

The top strip summarizes clients needing a message reply, upcoming meetings, stale applications, and clients without recent contact. The application pipeline board lets you drag cards to update application status without opening each record.

Screenshot: Application pipeline and Today metrics

Caseload table

Each row shows service, stage, message status, natural supports, and IJP actions when applicable. Clients who need follow-up sort toward the top.

Column Purpose
Name Open client detail; triage icons show alerts (no contact, etc.)
Messages “Needs reply” when the client is waiting on you
Support Natural supports workflow
IJP Collect IJP signature for GA Individual Job Placement clients during the monthly capture window

3. Client detail page

From a client name, you manage stage, job start date, applications, contact logs, meetings, activity timeline, vocational exports (when enabled), and staff-only notes (role permitting).

Contact & employment goals (client profile)

Update address, phones, and employment goals. Employer match suggestions appear when goals and location are complete.

Screenshot: Client profile form and employer matches

IJP monthly attestation

For IJP clients in the capture window, use Collect IJP signature on the caseload or the IJP monthly attestation section on the client page. Have the client sign on the pad (or upload a signed scan / blank PDF workflow). Saved attestations flow to billing; demo clients do not affect production billing flags.

Screenshot: IJP signature pad and Save IJP signature

4. Messages

Secure messaging with assigned clients. Reply promptly when the caseload shows “Needs reply.”

5. Billable hours (weekly timesheet)

Log client billable time by activity type, client, and duration. This is separate from the staff Time Clock (payroll). Supervisors and admins with caseload assignments can appear in the team member picker when viewing others’ timesheets.

Screenshot: Weekly timesheet with client and activity rows

6. Resources (when enabled)

  • Community Partners — partner network lookup
  • Explore Analytics — caseload and activity metrics
  • Download Exports — CSV and report exports
  • Submit Reports — external reporting portal link

7. Training demo checklist

  1. Portal → Settings → Demos → Launch ES training demo
  2. Review Today strip and pipeline on Clients
  3. Open a demo client — update profile fields and save
  4. Log a contact and a billable time entry
  5. Practice IJP signature capture on an IJP demo client (if seeded on GA IJP service)
  6. Exit demo from the green banner when finished
